    Controls

    PrivacySource

    Mouse and touch

    Pull the can, let go
    launch; the speed and direction you release with set the swing
    Tap the board
    catch the can
    Esc while pulling
    cancel the pull
    Drag an edge or corner
    resize the board
    Drag the brass handle (top edge)
    move the board under the pendulum, paint and all (Shift-drag on the board does the same)
    Drag the dashed ring round the can at rest
    move the pivot over the board; the board and its paint stay put
    Pull the steel handle (left edge), let go
    swing the board as the second pendulum; its period is the Board string slider. Pull it straight down for a vertical sway, as in the Lissajous example
    Right or middle drag, two fingers
    pan the view: board, pivot and can together
    View button
    the picture alone: rig, tray and readouts all go; tap or any key brings them back
    Movie mode
    on by default: five seconds after a pattern starts with nothing touched, the same happens by itself; move the mouse, touch or press a key to come back
    Double-click the board
    full screen
    Drag the tray grip
    move the tray; drop it near an edge to dock, double-click the grip to reset
    Theatre
    sit back: patterns compose themselves on a board held still, full screen, nothing else on show. Move for the bar: Next, Pause, Save, Studio. Keys there: n or → next, p pause, Esc back to the studio
    Surprise me
    a pattern composed for you: random family, colours, periods and release moment. Auto-play keeps them coming; on the phone bar, double-tap Surprise for auto-play and tap again to stop
    Example menu
    watch a pull demonstrated, then the pattern; a card lists the settings and gestures to repeat it by hand
    Swing chips
    hover to see one swing alone; click to recolour or remove it
