      Controls

      PrivacySource

      Mouse and touch

      Pull the can, let go
      launch; the speed and direction you release with set the swing
      Tap the board
      catch the can
      Esc while pulling
      cancel the pull
      Drag an edge or corner
      resize the board. On a touch screen the corners are drawn as small brass grips, and the whole edge is a wide target
      Drag the brass handle (top edge)
      move the board under the pendulum, paint and all (Shift-drag on the board does the same)
      Drag the dashed ring round the can at rest
      move the pivot over the board; the board and its paint stay put
      Turn with the pivot off the board's centre
      the board turns about its own centre, like a turntable on an axle, so the swing's loops walk round in a ring and leave a hole (the Turntable example)
      Pull the steel handle (left edge), let go
      swing the board as the second pendulum; its period is the Board string slider. Pull it straight down for a vertical sway, as in the Lissajous example
      Right or middle drag, two fingers
      pan the view: board, pivot and can together
      View button
      the picture alone: rig, tray and readouts all go; tap or any key brings them back
      Movie mode
      on by default: five seconds after a pattern starts with nothing touched, the same happens by itself; move the mouse, touch or press a key to come back
      Double-click the board
      full screen
      Drag the tray grip
      move the tray; drop it near an edge to dock, double-click the grip to reset
      Phone bar
      Clear, Undo, Surprise, Save, Theatre, More. Tap the board to catch the can. Surprise and the Examples start on a clean board; two Undos bring the old picture back
      If the board wanders off
      a button appears in the middle of the screen to bring it back. Fit board (in the tray) also squares the board and recentres the view
      Set as screen saver (Android app)
      opens the phone's Screen saver settings; choose Paint Pendulum there and when it should start (charging, docked)
      Theatre
      sit back: patterns compose themselves on a board held still, full screen, nothing else on show. Move for the bar: Next, Pause, Save, Studio. Keys there: n or → next, p pause, Esc back to the studio
      Surprise me
      a pattern composed for you: random family, colours, periods and release moment. Auto-play keeps them coming; on the phone bar, double-tap Surprise for auto-play and tap again to stop
      Example menu
      watch a pull demonstrated, then the pattern; a card lists the settings and gestures to repeat it by hand
      Swing chips
      hover to see one swing alone; click to recolour or remove it
      Gallery: How to
      shows the recipe under the picture without leaving the gallery; press it again to hide it
      Coil (slider)
      how thickly the paint is poured. Turned up, the falling stream buckles and coils like a rope of syrup on a plate: tight loops where the swing dawdles at its ends, pulled straight through the fast middle. A pen never coils
      Signed
      a small signature sits in the board's corner and on every saved picture, as a painting is signed; untick it in the tray for a blank board

      About

      Paint Pendulum was made by Dusko Delic in Teddington, London, in September 2026. It is a real spherical pendulum on a real second pendulum, not a drawn curve, which is why no two swings quite repeat. It is free, carries no adverts and needs no account; the code is open under the MIT licence. More toys at duskod.github.io.
