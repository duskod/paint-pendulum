Privacy

Paint Pendulum · pendulum.clockerly.com · last updated 7 September 2026

Paint Pendulum is a toy that runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. Nothing you make in it is uploaded anywhere.

Your paintings

Paintings exist only on your screen while the page is open. Save renders the board on your device and hands the file to your phone's own share sheet or download folder. The app never receives a copy.

Settings

Your custom colours, desk, board size and position, tray position, and which room you were last in are remembered in your browser's local storage, on your device only.

Permissions

The app uses no camera, microphone, location or motion sensors. It may ask to go full screen, which only changes how it is displayed.

Usage counts

The site does not currently run any analytics. If a privacy-friendly visit counter is added later, it will set no cookies and record no identifiers, and this page will say so.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/duskod/paint-pendulum.
